      About this page

      What counts as new?

      A title is new when the system sees it for the first time after it started on 11 October 2026. A title is updated when its details changed later, for example when a conference date was added.

      Titles that were already registered when the system started are stored, but they are not shown as new.

      Where the data comes from

      • Crossref, the register of publications: all five publishers.
      • IEEE Xplore: IEEE conferences and journals.
      • Springer Nature API: Springer volumes, often before Crossref has them.

      The detection time is the time from Crossref registering a title to its first report here. It is limited by the nightly schedule.

      For developers

      • Atom feed of every change event (new and updated titles).
      • JSON API: the data behind this page, for scripts and your own charts.
